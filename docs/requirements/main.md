We are **NovaTech Solutions Ltd.**, a software development and IT consulting company based in Bangladesh. We provide custom software development, ERP implementation, website development, mobile application development, UI/UX design, cloud deployment, and annual maintenance services for our clients.

Our company has grown significantly over the last few years. We currently manage most of our operations using spreadsheets, email, WhatsApp, Google Drive, and various third-party tools. As our client base continues to grow, these manual processes are becoming difficult to manage. We are looking for a centralized ERP solution that can manage our entire business from lead generation to project delivery, invoicing, payment collection, customer support, and employee management.

The system should be web-based, support multiple users with different roles and permissions, maintain a complete audit trail of every activity, and allow us to generate reports whenever required.

## Client Management

We need a complete CRM where our sales team can manage all prospects and existing clients.

The system should allow us to:

* Store company information
* Store multiple contact persons for a client
* Record meetings
* Record phone calls
* Save meeting notes
* Upload business documents
* Maintain communication history
* Assign clients to sales executives
* Record lead sources (Facebook, Website, LinkedIn, Referral, Exhibition, Cold Call, etc.)
* Schedule follow-up activities
* Receive reminders before follow-up dates
* Track every stage of the sales pipeline

Each lead should eventually be converted into a customer without losing previous communication history.
